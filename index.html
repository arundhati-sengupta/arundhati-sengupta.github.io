Hi, I'm Arundhati Sengupta

I'm a computational linguist working on LLM data, annotation and evaluation
Resume Research Contact

I build the language data that models learn from, and I test what they get wrong. My work spans many languages, scripts and dialects, from annotation guidelines to systematic LLM evaluation, across both speech and text.

I hold a Ph.D. in Linguistics from the Graduate Center, City University of New York, where I also taught NLP-focused courses. My dissertation studied Bengali grapheme-to-phoneme conversion and included the first systematic evaluation of large language models on Bengali G2P.

Before that, I spent seven years at the Central Institute of Indian Languages leading multilingual speech and text corpus projects, evaluated speech data and acoustic and phonemic models at Google, and did linguistic QA for Lionbridge. I'm a native speaker of English and Bengali and work professionally in Hindi.

What I work on

From collecting the data to measuring the model.

LLM evaluation

Testing what models get wrong

Zero- and few-shot prompt design, API-based benchmarking against task baselines, and categorizing model errors by type and root cause to guide prompt design and model selection.

Data & annotation

Building data people can trust

Annotation guideline authoring, annotator training and QC, inter-annotator agreement, and large-scale multilingual corpus curation with native-speaker annotators.

Rules & models

Encoding linguistic knowledge

Grapheme-to-phoneme and diacritization models in Python and PyTorch, plus regular-expression and finite-state (WFST) rules for normalization and rule-based classification.
