Research

How people write, how machines read. My research looks at the gap between written text and spoken language, and at how well models, from neural sequence models to LLMs, bridge it.

Dissertation

A Study of Bengali Grapheme-to-Phoneme Conversion

Arundhati Sengupta · Ph.D. in Linguistics, Graduate Center, City University of New York, 2026

Bengali is spoken by over 230 million people, yet G2P conversion for it has received little attention. Bengali spelling doesn't map neatly onto pronunciation, above all in whether the inherent vowel is pronounced and how. My dissertation identified where G2P errors come from and developed ways to fix them.

  • Neural model comparison and error analysis. I trained and compared four neural G2P architectures (attentive recurrent models, a transducer and a transformer) and used a finite-state error analysis to trace most remaining errors to inherent-vowel realization.
  • Hybrid models. Based on that finding, I built an inherent-vowel correction classifier that works as a post-hoc layer on top of the neural models and substantially reduces word error rate.
  • Multi-source corpus. I created the first multi-source G2P corpus for Bengali, combining two independently built lexicons, and found that a training resource's transcription consistency matters more than its raw coverage.
  • LLM evaluation. In the first systematic evaluation of LLMs on Bengali G2P, I piloted four models (Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude Opus 4.5, Claude Sonnet 4 and GPT-4o) through their APIs, using curated 20-shot prompts, then tested the best one, Gemini 2.5 Pro, in zero- and few-shot settings on two lexicons. Even Gemini trailed a purpose-built LSTM baseline (39.5 vs. 15.8 word error rate), and it failed most often on phonology the spelling doesn't show, such as inherent-vowel realization and diphthongs.
  • Diacritization. I introduced the first publicly available Bengali diacritization corpus, and showed that restoring pronunciation-disambiguating diacritics lets both a recurrent model and a fine-tuned byte-level transformer resolve the ambiguity directly.

Selected projects

Tools, corpora and earlier research.

G2P Error Analysis Tool

A WFST- and rule-based QA tool that automates quality control and systematic error reporting on grapheme-to-phoneme model output.

Grant-funded

Multilingual annotated corpora

Large-scale corpora built with native-speaker annotators, with guidelines I wrote and ambiguous cases I adjudicated (BSF grant). Integrated with heterogeneous datasets in SQL and Python (PSC-CUNY grant).

M.A. thesis

Personality Trait Prediction from Speech
